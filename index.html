Gandi Estates (40-Unit Residential Campus)

North Main Street & Grassy Hill Road, Waterbury, CT

The Geographic Reality: Waterbury, historically known as the "Brass City," presents significant topographical challenges for modern developers. Nestled in New Haven County, the region is notorious for shallow bedrock—requiring complex ledge rock mitigation—and strict environmental oversight.

Our Execution: C&L Development successfully navigated the stringent Inland Wetlands and Watercourses Commission (IWWC) to protect the Chase Brook buffer zones. By leveraging the city's RS-12 zoning regulations, we engineered a phased, rolling-block vertical construction strategy. This direct multi-prime CM model compressed the owner's original $25M market expectation down to a highly controlled $17.37M definitive capital cap.
